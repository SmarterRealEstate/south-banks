The Project

Where the City
Meets the Shore

South Banks is an exceptional new collection of pre-construction 3-storey townhomes at Lakeshore Rd E & Hydro Rd in Mississauga's transformative Lakeview Village community. Over 120 spacious homes designed for modern waterfront living — steps from the marina, pier, and park.

As part of the 177-acre Lakeview Village master plan, South Banks puts you at the heart of one of the GTA's most ambitious waterfront redevelopments — where former industrial lands are becoming a complete lakeside neighbourhood with 8,050 homes, 50+ acres of parks, and a 600-metre pier with retail boardwalk.

Unit types include 3-bedroom, 3-bedroom plus den, 4-bedroom, and end units. All with private entrances, layered outdoor spaces, floor-to-ceiling windows, EV charging capabilities, and smart home infrastructure.
